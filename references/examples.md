# Dialogue Examples for AI漫剧

## Example 1: Confession Scene (Before → After)

**Before (stiff):**
男主：我喜欢你。
女主：我也喜欢你。

**After (improved):**
【场景：雨夜天台】
男主：（把伞倾斜，自己肩头湿透）……你非要站这么远？
女主：（抱臂，声音发紧）伞是你的，我没资格用。
男主：（低笑一声）那我站过去行不行。
女主：（别过头）你站过去……也改变不了什么。

**Why better**: Subtext instead of direct confession; visual action (umbrella) that AI can generate; short lines; tension instead of flat agreement.

## Example 2: Argument with Character Voice

**霸总风格（克制、压迫） vs 女主（值强、不软）**

霸总：（解袖扣，声音平）合同你看过了。签。
女主：（把笔推回去）我不是来签字的。
霸总：（抬眼）那你是来浪费我时间的？
女主：（直视）我是来告诉你，这份合同有坑。你要是还当我是合作方，就先听完。

**Note**: 霸总用短句、命令式；女主用反驳+条件，保持性格。

## Example 3: Emotional Peak for Short Clip

Keep the strongest emotional line near the end of the 15-40s segment, paired with a clear facial expression or gesture that AI video tools can emphasize (tear, clenched jaw, forced smile, turning away).

## Quick Checklist Before Output
- [ ] Each character has a distinct speech rhythm
- [ ] At least one line uses subtext
- [ ] Lines are short enough for AI voice + short video
- [ ] Scene has a clear want/obstacle between characters
- [ ] Visual action or expression is suggested for key lines
